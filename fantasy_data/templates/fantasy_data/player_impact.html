Lost on the Bench

Losses where starting a benched player over the weakest starter he could replace would have won the game, and the team would still win even if the other side had played its best possible lineup too. When a game lists more than one player, any one of them would have done it.

A bad call means the benched player was projected to outscore the starter, so the warning was there before kickoff. A fair call means the projections liked the starter, so it was bad luck more than a bad decision. Projections are from Sleeper, scored half-PPR like the league.
